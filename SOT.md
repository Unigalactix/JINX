## 1. CORE STORY AND FIXED CANON

- **Title:** JINX.
- **Main timeline:** 2165-2180.
- **Ancient exception:** The prologue takes place on ancient Earth and connects to memories and evidence recovered in the main timeline.
- **Primary setting:** California. Other U.S. locations appear where needed.
- **Tone:** Dark science fiction, violent ancient combat, disaster, psychological struggle, moral conflict, and survival.
- **Technology:** Advanced future infrastructure, AI, neural mapping, clone-body growth, and speculative propulsion.

### Main character: Egon Stein, also called Ego.

- A scientist with 8 doctorates spanning space physics and botany-related fields.
- Has DID with 12 identities, including Egon.
- His life is troubled by discontinuity, exhaustion, and difficulty accepting help.
- By Chapter 3, therapy and cooperation improve his ability to manage symptoms and lead.
- This is ongoing work, not an instant cure or the erasure of the other identities.
- His motivation is to preserve people, knowledge, culture, and living systems rather than let everything humanity learned disappear.

### The lunar disaster

- In 2165, scientists discover that the Moon will devastate Earth within the next 15 years.
- The first 8 years provide the safe planning period for launching the interstellar fleet.
- After 2173, the Moon's motion becomes too unpredictable for reliable launch planning.
- From that point, collision could take another 3-7 years.
- In this draft, the Moon breaks and its fragments strike Earth on December 21, 2177.
- Earth is devastated but not physically split apart. Some people survive in deep, protected Earth refuges.

### The original people

- No original human travels to another solar system.
- All source volunteers remain on Earth.
- After the crash, originals are either dead, missing, or surviving on Earth.
- The surviving originals are not relocated to Mars or an off-world human colony.
- Their clones' lives do not mean the originals escaped death or continued as the same individual.

### REDO mission

- Five AI-operated ships carry 1,000 dormant clones each, plus protected consciousness records.
- The ships contain no conscious human passengers.
- Clone bodies remain in developmental stasis; the complete conscious neural patterns are not activated during transport.
- AI navigates, maintains the vessels, handles emergencies, and prepares safe destination habitats.
- Clones gain consciousness only after arrival and safety checks.
- Mercy's clones first awaken on Zan in the REDO system in September 2178.
- Other ships must activate their populations only at their respective destinations. Their last received packets confirm arrival and activation plans, not completed awakenings.
- Aster is an earlier experimental mission, not one of the five fleet ships. Its dormant prototypes awaken only after accidental arrival on ancient Earth.

### REDO geography

- REDO is 35 light-years from Earth. This is a distance, not a 35-year voyage.
- Speculative metric-compression propulsion permits an approximately six-year voyage within the requested timeline.
- Zan is the principal settlement planet; its immense ocean is Hades.
- Zan has exactly TWO moons: Agape and Ales.
- The epilogue describes the system's white primary Spica and distant red companion Antares.
- These are the fictional REDO survey names, not the distant Earth-sky stars whose names were borrowed.
- Casa, Judo, and Flack are other worlds known through surveys.
- Zan has 36-hour days.

## 2. ORIGINAL PROLOGUE AND BLOOD-RAIN INTENT

The prologue opens on a battlefield with a man carrying the sharpest blade known to mankind.
A swing can tear the air and cut people beyond the blade's physical reach.
Ten thousand defeated warriors have refused to merge their kingdom with the king's.
The king orders his one-man army to kill them.
The warrior promises that if anyone kills him, the rest will be freed; if anyone scratches him, he will put down the blade and leave.

The fighting remains violent and gory, including soldiers fleeing, hiding, resisting, and being pursued.
By sunset, all 10,000 have been killed.
The warrior stands before the king covered in blood, none of it his.
The blade remains sharp, clean, and unstained.
The prologue ends there. Events after that point are revealed later, not added beyond the prologue's intended ending.

The major modern scene is blood rain at Torrey Pines on the San Diego coast.
Egon stands on a sharp-edged rock while blood falls from the sky and mixes with the Pacific.
He receives flashes of the ancient killings and realizes the passage leads backward, not forward.
The blood appears to rise from the past into clouds before falling in California.
In the draft, this is matter transported through a spacetime anomaly, not ordinary evaporation.
The final clear-rain scene on Zan deliberately mirrors this moment.

### Original required subjects retained

- 666.
- Blood rain.
- Multiple identities.
- Space travel.
- A scientist as the main character.
- A violent ancient prologue.

## 4. MAIN CHARACTERS AND THEIR PURPOSE

### Egon Stein / Ego

- Role: Main scientist, REDO founder, and viewpoint character.
- Purpose: Move from control and self-reliance toward cooperation, responsibility, and preservation without ownership.
- Versions: Original Earth-side Egon, early prototype E-0, and later destination counterparts are different people.
- The Mercy/Zan counterpart keeps the name Egon after awakening.
- Epilogue purpose: Connect both families without taking sides; preserve the victims' history and demonstrate leadership without becoming a ruler.

### Helen Vale

- Role: Neurologist, medical lead, and Egon's close friend.
- Purpose: Protect consent, privacy, medical honesty, and the rights of the people the mission creates.
- Important distinction: She is not Egon's therapist.
- Original's last status: Survives in an Earth-based Sierra medical refuge.
- Zan counterpart: Awakens after arrival, builds a relationship with David, and becomes Theo's mother.
- Epilogue development: Her medical team assists Rak's birth; she later experiences childbirth herself. Care connects the two households despite their differing politics.

### David Mercer

- Role: Orbital dynamicist, navigation specialist, and investigator.
- Purpose: Establish the scientific threat, reconstruct Aster's fate, and argue for reliable shared safety systems.
- Original's last status: Works at a buried communications station in Nevada after the crash.
- Zan counterpart: Becomes Theo's father and advocates central coordination of life-support infrastructure.
- Epilogue motivation: Protect people from failures that spread through shared systems; he opposes independent release authority, not Rak's family as people.

### Salvador Vega

- Role: Pilot and flight-safety lead.
- Purpose: Supply practical judgment, humor, and the authority to stop unsafe operations.
- Original: Supervises fleet departure from Earth, never boards a starship, and later flies rescue craft on Earth.
- Zan counterpart: Awakens at the destination; no in-transit human piloting occurs.

### Sanya Rao

- Role: Botanist and ecological lead.
- Purpose: Preserve living systems, challenge simplistic ideas of saving knowledge, and prevent reckless colonization.
- Original: Sends advice during the coolant emergency and survives in an Earth seed refuge. Her delayed endorsement does not precede or authorize the ship's immediate emergency action.
- Zan counterpart: Learns what was lost during the journey and rebuilds the ecological collection.
- Personal motif: California poppy seeds from the place where her mother waited for a bus.

- Supervises departure from Earth and later coordinates coastal relocation.

- Dormant prototype during the local orbital test.

- Travels without consciousness in a dormant clone body.
- First awakens on Zan on September 3, 2178.
- Remembers the source's life through the final map, not an experienced voyage.
- Learns later events from records; does not automatically inherit the source's post-mapping experiences.

- Same separation applies to Helen, David, Salvador, Sanya, Mateo, and Leena.
- They may recognize one another through inherited memories but form new relationships after awakening.

### Rak and Theo

- Separate children born into separate families after the colony forms.
- Each begins with his own life, not an adult consciousness record.
- Their parents are first-generation awakened clones; the children are naturally born.

## 9. TECHNOLOGY AND PLOT MECHANISMS

### Project Recreation

- Grows clone bodies and stores complete neural maps as inactive information.
- Developmental stasis prevents conscious pattern activation before arrival.
- At a safe destination, medical AI installs and activates the stored patterns.
- This is fictional future technology, not a claim about present-day cloning or neuroscience.

### Metric-compression propulsion

- Shortens the route through space while the ship follows forward time.
- Enables an approximately six-year trip across the 35-light-year Earth-REDO separation.
- Its field damages an already active human conscious architecture.

### Other infrastructure

## 16. GORE, VIOLENCE, AND PERIL CHARTS

### Definitions

- **Gore:** Visible or described blood, wounds, dismemberment, and bodily damage. It measures graphic presentation, not the number of deaths.

- **Disaster/peril:** Threat to life from catastrophe, environmental exposure, or system failure. It is separate from intentional violence.
- All scores describe the **current written draft**, not targets for adding material. A non-graphic catastrophic event can have low gore and very high peril.
