01 The premise

Save what we know.
Not who we are.

The Moon is falling. Humanity has eight years to launch a future it may never see.

In California, scientist Egon Stein proposes REDO: five AI-operated ships carrying dormant clone bodies and the records of the people who made them. The originals remain on Earth. The counterparts will first become conscious at their destinations.

This is a story about memory, responsibility, and the difference between preserving a life and owning the one that follows.
